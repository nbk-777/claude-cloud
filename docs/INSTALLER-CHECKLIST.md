# Installer checklist (items the prototype cannot verify)

> This is not an installation instruction. Gas, flue, condensate, electrical and hydraulic work must be
> done by qualified people under local regulations: an electrician for the wiring, and an Ariston service
> technician for the boiler connections.

1. Confirm the 14 flow/return pairs. Trace each loop to its room, including any loop that crosses rooms. Enter the result in the app's mapping table and tick "verified".
2. Check that each 3-core thermostat cable is suitable and what each conductor does. The model assumes core 1 = L, core 2 = N, core 3 = signal.
3. Check the Beok thermostat terminal labels and output type (230 V switched live?) against the wiring-centre zone input.
4. Identify the exact boiler variant and GC number, and check the TA1 contact requirements. The GB manual (p.11) says 12 V DC with volt-free contacts only.
5. Verify that the wiring-centre relay COM/NO is a dry contact before connecting it to TA1. The factory jumper must be removed by the technician, and 230 V must never reach TA1.
6. Get approval for the balcony thermo-cabinet from Ariston and under local rules. Check frost (manual: +5 °C minimum ambient; anti-frost needs power, gas and pressure), condensate pipe freezing, CW/DHW pipe freezing, ventilation and flue.
7. Confirm the actuator voltage (230 V), NC type and M30×1.5 thread against the chosen manifold.
8. Check the wiring centre's per-zone and total load limits. The living zone carries 6 actuators, plus hall and wardrobe if they follow it.
9. Decide which zone the hall and wardrobe follow, or give them their own thermostats.
10. Check minimum flow and closed-zone behaviour, and whether the boiler's internal automatic by-pass is enough.
11. Decide whether a mixing unit, by-pass or thermostatic valve is needed, and how the floor temperature will be protected (FLOOR/TA2 safety thermostat, parameter 223).
12. Check that the boiler pump suits the loop lengths and pressure drops (GB p.15 graph).
13. Compare the system water volume with the 8 L expansion vessel.
14. Check DHW pipe length and the hot-water delay at the bathroom shower.
15. Check that the floor pipes have an oxygen barrier (DIN 4726/4729), or separate the circuits (GB p.11).
16. Choose the DIP delay (0 / 2 / 5 min) relative to the actuator opening time (≈3–5 min).
17. Leave pump output L1/N1 unused unless the hydraulic design needs it.
